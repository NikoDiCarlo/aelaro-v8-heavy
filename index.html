About Aelaro

Aelaro is a ranked disclosure changelog for public-market analysts. It scans the full S&P 500 in browser-orchestrated batches, ranks exact-date 10-K and 10-Q filings by likely analyst relevance, and generates source-backed changelogs for the top three.

It does not make investment recommendations, rank securities, issue price targets, or predict returns. It organizes public filing evidence into a clean review queue.

Terms of Use

Research use only. Aelaro provides research workflow support using public filings and AI-generated summaries.

  • No investment advice, trading recommendations, price targets, or buy/sell signals are provided.
  • Outputs may be incomplete or incorrect. Always verify against original SEC filings.
  • Users are responsible for their own research, compliance review, and decisions.
  • SEC EDGAR source links are provided where available for verification.
  • This v8-lite workflow depends on the browser tab remaining open while the batch scan runs.
